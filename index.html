From Bean to Bottle

Four clean steps — the reason our milk tastes fresh and stays safe.

1️⃣

Bean Selection

Only clean, high-grade soyabeans pass inspection. Damaged or poor-quality beans are rejected before processing.

2️⃣

Soaking & Grinding

Beans are soaked and ground under hygienic conditions to draw out maximum nutrition and a smooth texture.

3️⃣

Pasteurisation

The extracted milk is heated to safe temperatures, eliminating harmful bacteria while preserving taste and nutrients.

4️⃣

Sealed Packaging

Filled into sterilised, sealed containers and chilled immediately — then dispatched straight to your door.
